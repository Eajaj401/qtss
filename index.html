01 — Why QITS

Inspection is only useful if it's honest.

Our job is simple to describe and hard to do well: confirm that a product, weld or process actually meets the requirement it was supposed to meet, before it leaves the shop floor or the site. That's the whole point of third-party inspection — a second, independent set of eyes that isn't invested in looking the other way.

We work with a technical edge, keep it cost-effective, and keep our teams deployed across Eastern India so we're never far from where the work is happening. Along the way we help control quality, cut manufacturing costs, and reduce scrap and rework caused by defects that should have been caught earlier.

02 — What we do

Six services, one standard.

01

Quality Inspection & Survey

Checking, measuring and testing product and service characteristics against specification, carried out by skilled, verified and certified personnel — in line with national and international codes including IS, ASTM, DIN, BS and JIS.

02

Project Expediting

Coordinated expediting across every supplier — field and desk visits, production control, pre-dispatch monitoring, supplier performance tracking, and clear reporting so nothing surprises you at handover.

03

Supplier Management & Evaluation

Quantitative and qualitative assessment of potential suppliers against quality, cost, delivery, financial integrity and long-term fit — so the list you're left with is one you can actually trust.

04

NDT Testing Service

Non-destructive testing across visual, ultrasonic, magnetic particle, penetrant and radiographic methods — instruments and procedures recognised as standard for materials testing and welding services.

05

Welding Spec, Procedure & Welder Certification

WPS, PQR and WPQ drafting and documentation, welder training, in-process and visual inspection, PWHT procedure review, and mechanical test witnessing — closed out with a certified copy for your records.

06

On-Site Inspection & Manpower

Deployed specialists checking safety standards, working conditions and field compliance, plus sourcing for entry-level through senior technical and managerial roles across industrial sectors, short or long term.
